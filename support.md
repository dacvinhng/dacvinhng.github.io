# Shootile Support

Need help? Email **weyestudio@gmail.com**. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and a short description of the problem. Screenshots help a lot. We usually reply within two business days.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play?

Slide left or right to move your ship. Swipe down to grab the bottom run of one color (up to six blocks), then swipe up to shoot them into a column. Connect 4 or more blocks of the same color to clear them, and don't let the rows reach your ship.

### Can I use buttons instead of gestures?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear icon on the main menu) and choose Classic or Split Pad.

### How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, open the store (tap your Coins and Rubies balance on the main menu) and tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**. Remove Interstitial Ads, Full Puzzle Collection and Cosmic Premium Bundle are restored. Rubies are a consumable currency and cannot be restored.

### Is my progress saved when I change iPhones?

Progress is stored on your device. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one to keep it.

### Does "Remove Interstitial Ads" remove every ad?

It removes the automatic interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to revive) stay available because you choose when to watch them.

### Which puzzles are free?

The Starlight Mammoth collection is free. Full Puzzle Collection, or the Cosmic Premium Bundle, unlocks the other 25 prehistoric collections.

### How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking permission is in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In regions where it is required, the PRIVACY button on the main menu reopens the ad privacy options.

### How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open SUPPLY on the main menu and use the REMINDERS switch, or change Notifications for Shootile in iOS Settings.

### My scores don't appear in Game Center

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings and connected to the internet. Scores earned offline are sent the next time you are online.
