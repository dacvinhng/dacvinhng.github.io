# Shootile Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shootile game for iPhone ("Shootile" or "the app") handles information. Shootile is developed by Nguyen Dac Vinh ("we" or "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at weyestudio@gmail.com.

## Summary

- Shootile does not require an account, and we do not run servers that collect your personal information.
- Your game progress, settings and purchases are stored on your device.
- Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below. Where required by law you are asked for consent, and the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you decide whether your device's advertising identifier may be used to track you.

## Information stored on your device

Shootile stores your game progress, scores, Coin and Ruby balances, unlocked content, settings and reminder preferences locally on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app (it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device).

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Shootile shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required. Google may collect and process the following information from the app:

- Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and app- or developer-scoped identifiers
- Your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device
- Product interaction information, such as app launches, taps and video views
- Advertising information, such as the ads you have seen or interacted with
- Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data

Google uses this information to show, measure and improve ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and for analytics. Your choices:

- **App Tracking Transparency.** Shootile asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland.** Google's consent message lets you choose how your information is used for ads. You can review or change your choices later with the PRIVACY button on the main menu.
- **Some U.S. states.** Where privacy options are required, the PRIVACY button on the main menu lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising.

Ads in Shootile are limited to content suitable for the game's age rating. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one. The "Remove Interstitial Ads" purchase turns off interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Shootile uses Apple's StoreKit on your device to deliver and restore purchases. See the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores, achievements, challenges and activity progress are sent to Apple's Game Center service and may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. Game Center is operated by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple Account details.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Shootile schedules local notifications on your device. No notification information is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the game or in iOS Settings.

## Children

Shootile is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

## Keeping and deleting information

We do not keep personal information about you on our servers. Information stored by the app is removed when you delete the app. You can reset your advertising identifier and control tracking in iOS Settings. For information processed by Google, see [Google's privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not store personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, and requests about App Store or Game Center data to Apple. You can always contact us at weyestudio@gmail.com and we will help you.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
